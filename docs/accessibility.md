# Accessibility

The 57-second Cutver film ships in three versions. All of them are 1920x1080, 30 fps, 57.0 s,
H.264 video with AAC audio, and all three share the same picture, byte for byte. The original is
embedded in the [README](../README.md); the two described tracks sit behind a disclosure there and
are linked below.

| Version | What it is | Open |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original | Music only. No narration, no description. | [play](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5567bfea-a752-4ced-96ee-7fd793ce2562) |
| Audio description — English | The film described for blind and low-vision viewers. | [play](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/157d57c6-0fd8-4271-802a-c0f0fdcc0076) |
| Audiodescripción — español neutro | La película descrita para personas ciegas y con baja visión. | [play](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c6a17256-9b18-46d6-a53f-6e4de8504d79) |

The files are issue attachments rather than repository contents, so they do not add ~157 MB to the
git history. Opening one streams it; saving it gives you the file.

## How the described tracks are built

The description is a complete mix, not a voice stem laid over the original: the music ducks 8 dB
under speech with raised-cosine ramps of 300 ms in and 700 ms out, so the bed breathes instead of
being pushed down. Between insertions the music returns to full level. Both tracks are normalised to
-14 LUFS integrated with a true-peak ceiling of -1 dBTP, the same delivery target as the film.

Each insertion has a hard window derived from the edit, and was checked against the picture after
encoding: every one lands within 20 ms of its mark, and the voice sits at a measured lag of 0.0 ms
inside the mix.

The Spanish track describes a film whose on-screen text is English. That is deliberate: the
screenshots are real product output, so they are not translated. The one exception is the closing
line, which is the brand slogan and is quoted in English by an English voice.

## Rebuilding

The film and both tracks are generated by the pipeline in `cutver-video`, which is not part of this
repository. Regenerating them means uploading three new attachments and updating the URLs above.
